# Sound credits

Every note worm_instruments plays is a sample in this folder, named in `Manifest.json`. Each set says where it came
from in its `Source`; the recorded ones are listed here with their licences. They were cut, tuned (a correction in
cents is stored per sample, the audio itself is not re-pitched), levelled and encoded to OGG by
`worm_instruments_assets/Source/Sounds/build_sounds.py`.

## Recorded

- **VCSL, Grand Piano, Steinway B (CC0)** — the piano. Versilian Community Sample Library by Versilian Studios,
  https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL — Creative Commons Zero 1.0: free for any use, no attribution required (given
  anyway).
- **VSCO 2 CE, Solo Violin, Arco Vib (CC0)** — the violin. Versilian Studios Chamber Orchestra 2 Community Edition,
  https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE — Creative Commons Zero 1.0.

## Synthesised

- **synthesised by build_sounds.py** — rendered by the build script itself (Wormhole Studios): the acoustic guitar, a
  plucked string by Karplus-Strong through a wooden body.
